Description

Welcome to this spacious detached bungalow set on a private site in the peaceful countryside of Glanmire, offering an excellent balance of rural tranquillity and convenient access to nearby amenities.

Extending to approximately 168 sq.m., this generously proportioned home offers flexible family accommodation with excellent potential for updating and modernisation throughout.

The property is entered via a welcoming front porch leading into a bright and spacious interior. To the front of the home is a separate living room, creating a cosy and private reception space, while a large family room positioned centrally within the property provides an additional living area ideal for everyday family life and entertaining. The kitchen and adjoining dining area form the heart of the home, complemented by a separate utility/laundry room for added practicality.

The accommodation comprises six bedrooms in total, including a spacious primary bedroom positioned to the rear of the property. One of the bedrooms is currently being used for storage, offering flexibility for a home office, playroom, or additional guest accommodation. A long central hallway enhances the sense of space and connects the living and sleeping accommodation throughout the home. Multiple bathroom facilities further add to the practicality of the layout.

Externally, the property benefits from private parking and surrounding outdoor space with excellent potential to further enhance the gardens and exterior areas to suit individual tastes.

Heating is currently provided via a solid fuel back boiler system, while the option for oil-fired central heating is also available, although this would require reconnection.

While the property would benefit from refurbishment and modernisation, it presents a fantastic opportunity for buyers seeking to create a substantial family home in a scenic countryside setting.

Located just a short drive from Glanmire village and within easy reach of Cork City, the property enjoys the best of both worlds — peaceful rural living while remaining convenient to schools, shops, cafés, sporting facilities, and major road networks.
